Under the hood

Three steps, one model, sub-50ms per chunk.

01

Tokenize

Text splits into subword tokens via the DistilBERT tokenizer. Large content is chunked into overlapping segments.

02

Classify

Each chunk flows through a 6-layer transformer, fine-tuned on Civil Comments, exported to ONNX, quantized to INT8.

03

Score

Softmax normalizes logits into probabilities per chunk. The worst chunk determines the overall verdict.
